====== Roll Categories and List ======


  * D Files - Reports of Special Branch between 1929-1949  
  * IO Files - Reports made 1916-1929  
  * MIS Files  
  * N Files - Reports made 1943-1945 during the Japanese Occupation  
  * U Files - Reports made 1940-1945 during the Japanese Occupation  
  * W Files - Station W (Western area) files  
  * FMP Files - French Municipal Files  
  * BW Files - Bubbling Well Station Reports  
  * CID Files - Criminal Intelligence Division  
  * CS Files - Crime and Special Branch  
  * SMP File  
  * F Files - French Political Police  
  * FC Files - Film Censorship  
  * H Files - Headquarters Staff

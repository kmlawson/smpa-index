====== Resources of Use ======

  * [[https://www.virtualshanghai.net/|Virtual Shanghai]] - A large collection of materials related to Shanghai history.
  * [[https://froginawell.net/frog/primary-sources-history-of-modern-shanghai/|Primary Sources: History of Modern Shanghai]] - A collection of links to resources related to the history of modern Shanghai.
  * [[https://www.chinafamilies.net/records/shanghai-municipal-policemen/|Shanghai Municipal Policemen Searchable Database]] - There is a searchable database of many officers in the Shanghai Municipal Police at China Familes 
  * [[https://hpcbristol.net/exhibitions/shanghai-municipal-police-collections|Historical Photographs of China - Shanghai Municipal Police Collections]] - The Historical Photographs of China collection has assembled special collections with photographs that are found in the SMP records.

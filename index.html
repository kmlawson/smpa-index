Words are ANDed, and case and diacritics are ignored. Put a phrase in quotes, use -word to exclude a word, and use the prefixes title:, notes:, tags: and file: (e.g. file:D8039A, file:IO5) to search one field. Click a column heading to sort, or click a row for full details: every link, notes, page count and the books that cite the file. The filters and search are kept in the address bar, so a view can be bookmarked or shared.

Links. IA file is a scan of the individual file on the Internet Archive, usually the clearest copy. Roll 12 p.345 opens the full microfilm roll at that page; rolls sometimes include pages that are missing from the individual files. smp-files opens a fresh transcription and Chinese translation of the file on the smp-files project.

Auto-matched links. Most D-file links to individual files were added by matching the NARA folder titles against the titles of the Internet Archive items. Where the title also carries the D-number, or where the file's cover was checked, the details say so. Links marked auto are very likely right, but have not all been checked by hand; the details panel shows how each was matched.

Keep in mind that claims in these documents must be read with great care. SMP police records are full of stereotypes, rumour, gossip, slander and unsubstantiated claims about the people they report on.
